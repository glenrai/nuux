About NUUX

Built by people
who did the work.

GR
Glen Rai

Founder · Tallinn, Estonia

NUUX began with a simple observation. The people closest to a problem usually know what the right tool looks like, but rarely get to build it.

Our founder has spent years as an educator, an IB English examiner and an adviser on responsible AI. Each product grew out of that work. IO Examiner came from the classroom and the examination room. TIRA came from helping organizations make sense of the EU AI Act. In My Spheres came from the everyday need to show different people different sides of a working life.

We are based in Tallinn, and it shapes how we build: transparent logic, careful data handling and interfaces that respect people's time.
